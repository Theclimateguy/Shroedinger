# Phase 14 Protocol (preregistered): regime-dependent relaxation dynamics of the profile P

Status: FROZEN before any Phase-14 quantity was computed. Date frozen:
2026-08-16. Deviations logged with timestamp at the bottom.

## Motivation and scope

Phase 12 left the programme with one validated instrument and no dynamics:

- the profile `P` (spatial Spearman of adjacent log envelopes) responds to
  controlled irrecoverability (rho = -0.883 on the frozen ladder) and is, by
  construction, invariant to monotone amplitude transforms;
- the charge-discharge equation of Phase 6a for `A`-type quantities is NOT
  ESTABLISHED: roughly half its apparent relaxation was the memory of the
  W = 20 sliding estimator window, and its held-out predictive power was zero;
- Phase 5c integrability collapsed (7/48) once the surrogate null preserved
  temporal coherence.

Phase 14 asks the smallest dynamical question the surviving evidence
supports:

> Does `P_b(t)` obey a mean-reverting relaxation law whose equilibrium is
> regime-dependent,
>
> `dP_b/dt = -gamma_b (P_b - P_b^eq(regime_t)) + eta_b(t)`,
>
> where the regime is the convective state of the domain (CAPE), with
> parameters that (i) are not artifacts of the estimator window, (ii) are not
> shadows of field persistence, (iii) are not shadows of band amplitude, and
> (iv) transfer to held-out regions?

Two structural lessons from Phases 5c/6a/12 are built in as hard gates:

1. **No estimator window anywhere.** `P_b(t)` is an instantaneous spatial
   statistic (one snapshot per value). The relaxation rate is additionally
   required to be invariant to the fitting time step (H14b); if `gamma`
   changes with the step, the quoted relaxation is declared an estimator
   artifact, as in Phase 6a.
2. **Temporal-coherence-preserving nulls only.** Every coupling claim is
   scored against circular whole-day time shifts (which preserve both series'
   full autocorrelation and diurnal phase) and the estimator is pre-validated
   on phase-randomized surrogates of the real `P` series (which preserve the
   full power spectrum). An estimator that flags coupling on
   persistence-only surrogates invalidates the phase, as in Phase 12.

## Quantities (fixed)

All scale machinery is unchanged from Phase 2 (`ELLS_KM = [50, 100, 200,
400, 800, 1600]`, vorticity of 850 hPa wind, interior mask at 1600 km).

- `P_b(t)`, b = 0..4: spatial Spearman of adjacent log envelopes,
  `envelope_rho_profile` of Phase 2, per 6-h snapshot. No temporal window.
- Confirmatory composite: `P(t) = mean(P_3(t), P_4(t))` — the resolved
  200-800 km pairs fixed in Phases 9-12. Per-band results reported.
- Regime variable: `E(t)` = interior-mask mean of `log(1 + CAPE)`.
- Discharge covariate (secondary): `R(t)` = interior-mask mean of
  `log(precip + 1e-6)`.
- Amplitude placebo: `V(t) = mean_{i in {3,4}} log( interior mean D_i(t)^2 )`
  where `D_i = bar_i - bar_{i+1}` — the time-resolved analog of the band
  variance that defeated `A` in Phases 9-11.
- Deseasonalization (primary analysis): from each series, per region-window,
  remove (i) the hour-of-day mean (4 phases) and (ii) a linear trend. Raw
  series reported as a secondary column. `E` and `V` are z-scored per
  region-window after deseasonalization; `P` is left in correlation units
  but demeaned per region-window for pooled fits.

Discrete model fit by OLS per region-window (time step delta = 6 h):

```
DeltaP(t) = P(t+1) - P(t) = a + alpha * E(t) - gamma * P(t) + eta(t)
```

which is the Euler form of the target equation with
`P^eq = (a + alpha*E) / gamma`.

## Hypotheses and criteria (fixed)

- **H14a (estimator validity gate — decisive, computed first).**
  - *(i) Power.* For each fit region-window, synthetic `P*` series are
    generated by the discrete OU recursion driven by the REAL deseasonalized
    `E(t)` of that region-window, with known `gamma_true` in {0.05, 0.2,
    0.5} per step, equilibrium coupling `d = 0.5 sd(P*)` per sd of `E`,
    process noise matched to the real composite `P` variance, plus white
    measurement noise at 25% of the series sd; 20 replicates per cell.
    Requirement: median `|log(gamma_hat / gamma_true)| <= log(1.5)` for
    `gamma_true` in {0.2, 0.5}, and the H14c null test detects the coupling
    in >= 80% of replicates at `gamma_true = 0.2`.
  - *(ii) Specificity — the persistence gate.* 200 Fourier
    phase-randomized surrogates of the real composite `P` series per fit
    region-window (full power spectrum preserved, alignment with `E`
    destroyed), each scored by the H14c per-region-window null test at the
    5% level. Requirement: false-positive rate <= 10%. **If (ii) fails, the
    estimator reacts to persistence alone; nothing further in Phase 14 is
    interpreted, and the verdict is ESTIMATOR_INVALID.**
- **H14b (window/step invariance of the relaxation — form test).** Per
  region-window, `gamma_hat(k)` is estimated from the regression of
  `P(t+k)` on `P(t)` as `-log(slope_k) / k` for `k` in {1, 2, 4} steps
  (6 h, 12 h, 24 h). Criteria, on the composite band over all 32
  region-windows: (i) median of `max(gamma_hat)/min(gamma_hat)` over the
  three steps <= 2.0; (ii) median R^2 of the linear fit of `log ACF(k)`
  against `k` for k = 1..8 >= 0.90. Failure means the relaxation rate is an
  artifact of the fitting step — the Phase-6a disease in new clothes — and
  the form is rejected.
- **H14c (regime coupling, in-sample, against the coherent null).** Per fit
  region-window, `alpha_hat` from the OLS above; null distribution from 500
  circular time shifts of `E` by whole days (shift >= 20 days, both
  directions, diurnal phase preserved). Per-region-window pass:
  `|alpha_hat|` > 95th percentile of `|alpha_null|`. Criteria: (i) >= 12/16
  fit region-windows pass; (ii) among passing region-windows, the sign of
  `alpha_hat` is the same in >= 12; (iii) pooled `|alpha|` (all fit
  region-windows stacked, one shift draw per region-window per null
  replicate) > 95th percentile of its null. All three required. No sign is
  preregistered; the direction is an outcome, not an assumption.
- **H14d (held-out transfer — the criterion that killed Phase 6a).** Pooled
  coefficients `(a, alpha, gamma)` from the 16 fit region-windows, frozen.
  On each validation region-window: one-step MSE of the frozen full model
  vs a per-region-window AR(1)-only model `(a, gamma)` fit ON that
  validation series. Pass: frozen full model wins (strictly lower MSE) in
  >= 12/16 validation region-windows.
- **H14e (amplitude placebo).** Pooled placebo model `(a, p*V, gamma)` fit
  identically on the fit set. On each validation region-window: one-step
  MSE of the CAPE model vs the placebo model, both frozen from the fit set.
  Pass: CAPE model strictly better in >= 12/16. (If both models fail H14d
  there is nothing to compare; H14e is then scored failed.)
- **H14f (residual whiteness).** Full-model residuals per region-window,
  subsampled to daily (every 4th step, offset 0), Ljung-Box at 10 lags.
  Pass: p > 0.01 in >= 16/32 region-windows. Failure does not veto coupling
  but caps the verdict at PARTIAL: the OU form is then an incomplete
  description of the noise.

Reported, **descriptive only, no criterion weight**:

- equilibrium monotonicity: within-region-window CAPE terciles vs mean `P`;
- the precip covariate `R` added to the model (sign counts only);
- window-level ENSO contrast on `data/b2b` + `data/b3` (R5-R12): same-season
  sign tests of window-median composite `P`, JFM: {W5, W7 (La Nina)} vs
  {W11 (El Nino)}; JAS: {W6, W8 (La Nina)} vs {W10 (El Nino onset), W12};
  n = 8 regions per comparison — far too small to be more than descriptive;
- per-band (b = 0..4) versions of H14b-H14e statistics.

## Verdict rule (fixed)

- **ESTIMATOR_INVALID**: H14a(ii) fails. Stop.
- **FORM_REJECTED**: H14a passes, H14b fails. `P` does not relax at a
  step-invariant rate; no gamma may be quoted.
- **NO_REGIME_COUPLING**: H14a, H14b pass; H14c fails. `P` relaxes, but its
  equilibrium shows no CAPE dependence distinguishable from the coherent
  null. The equation reduces to `dP/dt = -gamma (P - P^eq) + eta` with a
  static regional `P^eq`.
- **COUPLING_NOT_TRANSFERABLE**: H14a-c pass; H14d or H14e fails. The
  coupling exists in-sample but either does not transfer across regions or
  is not distinguishable from amplitude. Announced exactly so.
- **P_DYNAMICS_ESTABLISHED**: H14a-e pass, H14f passes.
- **P_DYNAMICS_PARTIAL**: H14a-e pass, H14f fails.

## Deviations

- **2026-08-16, one post-hoc diagnostic added AFTER the battery was scored,
  declared diagnostic, no criterion weight**
  (`results/experiment_B14_p_relaxation/diagnostic_daily_steps.json`).
  H14b failed by a hair (median log-ACF R^2 = 0.8905 against the 0.90 bar;
  the step-ratio criterion passed at 1.34). To locate the failure, the
  step-invariance scan was repeated at daily lags (k = 4, 8, 12) and the
  log-ACF linearity on k = 4..16. Result: worse, not better (ratio 1.94,
  R^2 0.77), because the median ACF of P is already ~0 beyond 48 h
  (0.61 at 6 h, 0.34 at 12 h, 0.15 at 24 h) — beyond two days there is no
  signal left to be exponential about. The reading of FORM_REJECTED is
  therefore: relaxation is real, fast (tau ~ 12 h) and near-exponential at
  6-24 h, but a single-timescale OU is not certified, and no slow
  component exists in the deseasonalized fluctuations. The frozen verdict
  stands unchanged.
