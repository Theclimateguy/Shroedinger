# Legacy manuscript (superseded)

This folder preserves, for provenance only, the earlier manuscript of this
project (`main.pdf`, `main_ru.pdf` and the `manuscript/` LaTeX sources),
archived separately as [Zenodo 19565805](https://zenodo.org/records/19565805).

**Status of its claims.** The central empirical claim of that text — a local
`Lambda_b ~ Pi_b` closure in the ERA5 inertial range — was re-tested under the
preregistered program in this repository and **was not confirmed**: the
quantity used as a proxy for the spectral energy flux does not track the flux
computed by coarse-graining, and the reported statistic was inflated by
binning and by in-sample sign alignment. See `docs/RECONCILIATION.md` for the
authoritative scoreboard and `docs/RESEARCH_PROGRAM_FULL.csv` for the status
of every individual experiment.

The descriptors that survived the program, and the results that stand, are
documented in the repository root — they do not depend on this text.
